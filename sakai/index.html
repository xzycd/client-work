A fly in Baltic amber

Diptera · Eocene · 11 × 6 mm

Sakai cuts amber in Nida, around whatever the resin kept. Scroll to go back to the day it was caught.

Back to the resin

All Baltic amber started as pine resin. This fly landed on a fresh drop about 44 million years ago.

The drop closes

Resin moves slowly. It ran over the fly and kept it in this pose ever since.

Washed up below Nida

The forest is long gone under the sea. Storms still throw pieces onto this coast, and we pick them out of the seaweed.
