# Look: Sakai

Fictional amber workshop in Nida, Lithuania. Portfolio piece, not a real business.

## Default look
Vibe: museum macro. One specimen, lit from behind, nothing else in frame.
Light and colour: amber backlight on black; low gold sun through a pine forest; dawn on the Baltic shore.
Camera: real photograph, 5:1 macro for the specimen, 35 mm low on the sand for the shore.
World: the specimen, the Eocene pine bark, the beach below the dunes.
People and styling: none.
Formats: 16:9 stills at 2k, 5 s clips at 720p, scrubbed as frames.
Never: jewellery settings, hands, text.

## Products
### The specimen
Keep-list: one small fly seen from the side, wings folded back, legs tucked, dead centre, inside a polished honey-orange amber oval with a few air bubbles and one crack.

Seedance 2.0 Mini from the living-fly still gave a clean resin-swallows-fly shot. All five jobs were keepers on the first run.
