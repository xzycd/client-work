How this atlas works

Curated for the story, sourced for the facts.

Every story is condensed from the linked Wikipedia article or an official city and museum source. Dates and names stay factual; the writing is edited for the detail you would actually retell.

The 3D city is an interpretive model based on real coordinates. It gives you spatial bearings, not survey-grade building geometry. Photography comes from Wikimedia Commons with creator and license shown on each story.

Vilnius and Klaipėda have more histories than one atlas can hold. This edition favors places where architecture, memory, infrastructure, and reinvention collide.
