Practice

What we take on, and roughly how long it runs

You work with the people who do the work. There is no account layer to get through.

Two to four of us on a project, one project at a time. We write production code and we hand over the source files, the variable fonts, and the licences. Nothing is held back as leverage.

  • Brand identity and systems

    3–9 months

    Positioning through rollout, including the governance document nobody enjoys writing.

  • Typeface design

    4–12 months

    Latin and Latin Extended, variable or static. Drawn in-house, hinted and tested on real hardware.

  • Product and interface

    3–18 months

    Design systems, web, iOS. We ship components, not screenshots of components.

  • Art direction

    Per issue

    Print and screen, commissioning photographers and illustrators, on retainer or per project.

  • Implementation review

    2–6 weeks

    Somebody else made the brand and it is drifting. We audit what shipped and write the fix.

We turn down more work than we take.

Not out of principle. There are six of us, the work takes as long as it takes, and we would rather say no in March than apologise in October. If the timing is wrong we will usually say so in the first call and point you somewhere better.

Ana Sequeira and Tom Reddick, partners

Tell us what you are building.

Send a paragraph, not a brief. What it is, when it needs to be live, and a budget range if you have one. We read everything and answer within two working days, including the noes.
