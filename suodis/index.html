Suodis

A log sauna on a lake in southern Lithuania. It has no chimney, so the smoke leaves through the walls.

Suodžiai02Soot

Birch burns in the stove for six hours. Then the hatch opens and the smoke goes out, but the heat stays in the stones and the soot stays on the walls.
