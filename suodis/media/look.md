# Look: Suodis

Fictional smoke-sauna house on a lake in Dzūkija, Lithuania. Portfolio piece, not a real business.

## Default look
Vibe: a working smoke sauna in deep winter, shot like a quiet documentary still. Heat and cold in one frame.
Light and colour: blue hour and low winter daylight outside; inside, near-black soot with one beam of window light and a dull ember glow. Palette: soot black, ember orange, steam white, ice blue-grey, birch green as the only living colour.
Camera: real photograph, 35 mm (50 mm for close-ups), eye level or low on the ice, fine grain, no wide-angle distortion. Clips: tripod or slow glide, no handheld shake.
World: one low log sauna with no chimney, a frozen lake, pine and birch.
People and styling: none. No bodies, no hands, no faces (sauna plus skin trips the nsfw filter, and hands break).
Formats: 16:9 stills at 2k, 5 s clips at 720p, scrubbed by scroll as frame sequences.
Never: chimney, metal or electric stove, text, signage, logos, modern objects, puddles or wet look outdoors.
